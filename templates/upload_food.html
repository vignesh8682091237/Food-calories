AI-Based Food Calorie Estimation

Our AI-based system uses advanced image processing and machine learning algorithms to estimate the calorie content of the food you consume. Here's how it works:

  • Image Preprocessing: The uploaded food image is resized, normalized, and cleaned to enhance recognition.
  • Object Detection: A CNN model identifies and segments food items in the image.
  • Feature Extraction: Key features like shape, size, and texture are extracted to classify food items.
  • Calorie Estimation: The classified food items are matched against a pre-trained database of calorie values.
  • Results: The system displays the estimated calorie count along with nutritional information.

This technology helps track your diet, maintain a healthy lifestyle, and achieve your fitness goals!
